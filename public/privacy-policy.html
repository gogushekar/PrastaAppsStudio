PRIVACY
POLICY.

Last updated: May 2025

Prasta Apps Studio is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information our apps collect, how we use it, and your rights as a user.

01 Information We Collect

Our apps are designed to be lightweight and privacy-friendly. Depending on the specific app, we may collect:

  • Usage Data — Anonymous information about how you use the app (e.g. features used, session length) to help us improve.
  • Device Information — Basic device details such as OS version and device model for compatibility and crash reporting.
  • Ad Data — If the app shows ads via Google AdMob, Google may collect data in accordance with their own privacy policy.

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any personally identifiable information unless you explicitly provide it.

02 How We Use Information

Any information collected is used solely to:

  • Improve app performance and fix bugs
  • Understand which features are most useful
  • Display relevant advertisements through Google AdMob
  • Ensure compatibility across different Android devices

We do not sell, trade, or rent your information to any third parties.

03 Google AdMob & Third-Party Ads

Some of our apps use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may use cookies and device identifiers to serve personalised ads based on your interests.

You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time through your device settings:

  • Go to Settings → Google → Ads
  • Enable "Opt out of Ads Personalisation"

For more details, see Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

04 Data Storage & Security

Most of our apps store data locally on your device only. We do not operate our own servers for storing user data. Any crash reports or analytics are handled by trusted third-party services (such as Firebase) under their own security standards.

Your data stays on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

05 Children's Privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it immediately.

06 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of our apps after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
