POLICY.

App: MyNotes — Quick & Simple Package: com.mynotes.quicksimple Developer: Prasta Apps Studio Last updated: May 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how MyNotes (the Android application packaged as com.mynotes.quicksimple; debug builds may use com.mynotes.quicksimple.debug) (“App”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the App.

✉️

Contact

Prasta Apps Studio — privacy inquiries via the Google Play developer page or the support option on the MyNotes Play listing.

— Summary

  • Notes and notebooks are stored primarily on your device.
  • If you choose to sign in with Google, we sync an encrypted-in-transit snapshot of your notebooks and notes to Google Firebase / Firestore associated with your Google account, for backup and restore.
  • The App uses Google AdMob to show advertisements.
  • Firebase may provide crash, diagnostic, and analytics-related services depending on SDK configuration.

01 Information the App processes

1.1 Information you enter in the App

DataTypical use
Note content (titles, bodies, checklist text, reminders, notebook names, styling metadata) Core note-taking functionality, stored locally; optional cloud backup and restore

1.2 Device and usage data

DataWhy
Firebase installation / app instance identifiers used by Firebase SDKs App configuration and Firebase services (Analytics, Messaging if enabled, Installation IDs, etc.—per Firebase documentation)
Advertising identifiers and ad-related telemetry Delivering and measuring ads through Google AdMob (subject to Google’s policies and your device settings)
Technical logs/diagnostics on your device Debugging and security (mostly visible to developers via Android Studio/logcat—not sent to our servers unless a third-party SDK does so according to its own policy, e.g. Google/Firebase)

Exact categories depend on Firebase/Play Services SDK versions—see the Google Firebase Privacy Policy and Google Privacy & Terms.

1.3 Optional cloud backup (Google Sign-In)

If you tap sign in / cloud backup:

  • Firebase Authentication may process identifiers linked to your Google account (e.g. account email/UID shown in the SDK) solely to authenticate you.
  • Cloud Firestore stores documents representing your notebooks and notes under a path keyed to your Firebase user ID, so backup/restore works across installs.

Signing out stops new authenticated access from this installation until you sign in again. Deleting documents in Firebase follows the logic you trigger in the App (for example restore from backup, manual backup behaviour, or permanent delete flows as implemented).

1.4 Local storage and backups you control

You may export/import notes (e.g. as files). Files you export are controlled by you and your chosen sharing targets (Downloads, messaging apps, USB, etc.). We do not receive those exports unless you choose to upload them elsewhere.

02 How we use information

We use the above information solely to:

  • Provide note-taking, reminders, notebooks, trash/archive, search, templates, export/import
  • Optionally provide authenticated cloud backup/restore (Firebase Auth + Firestore)
  • Show advertisements (AdMob) and measure their performance subject to policies
  • Operate and improve Firebase-related SDK features as configured

We do not sell your personal note content as a product. Third-party ad and analytics frameworks may collect data under their own policies—see section 04.

03 Legal bases (EEA / UK users)

Where GDPR/UK GDPR applies, we rely on:

  • Contract / steps prior to contract — providing the note features you requested
  • Legitimate interests — secure operation, preventing abuse where proportionate, basic product analytics tied to Firebase as configured, ad-funded free tier (each subject to overriding rights and objections where applicable)
  • Consent — where platforms or laws require consent (e.g. certain ads/personalisation settings on your device)

05 Data retention

  • On device: persists until you uninstall the App, clear storage, or delete data inside the App. Purge-after-period rules implemented in-app (for example automated trash pruning) affect device retention only unless changes are synced to cloud per your flows.
  • In Firebase (if you use backup): notebook/note documents persist until overwritten, deleted according to App actions, or you contact us to request account/data deletion where applicable.

06 Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age defined in your country). If you discover a child supplied personal information, contact us—we will assist with deletion consistent with applicable law.

07 Security

We use HTTPS/TLS-protected Google/Firebase APIs provided by Firebase SDKs. No backend run by us receives your notes, except indirectly through Firebase under Google’s Terms. Perfect security does not exist—protect device access and your Google/Firebase account.

08 International transfers

Google may process Firebase/Ads data in multiple countries. See the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase data processing disclosures.

09 Your choices

You can:

  • Decline cloud sign-in — backups to Google won’t authenticate as you (offline/local use retained).
  • Sign out in the App.
  • Reset ad personalization via Google / device Settings (see Google Help).
  • Request Firebase/Google account data, export, or deletion per Google tooling where applicable, or contact us via the Play listing.
