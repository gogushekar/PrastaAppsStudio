— What “account” means in MyNotes

MyNotes does not use a separate username/password. If you used Sign in with Google for cloud backup, your account is your Google-linked Firebase account used to store backup data in Google Firebase (Firestore).

You can use MyNotes without signing in; in that case there is no cloud account, only data on your device.

See also our MyNotes Privacy Policy.

A Delete data on your device only (no cloud account)

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → MyNotes → Storage.
  2. Tap Clear data (or uninstall the app).

Deleted: notes, notebooks, and app preferences on that device.

Not deleted: nothing in the cloud (you were not signed in).

B Remove cloud backup content yourself (signed in)

  1. Open MyNotes → menu (☰) → Sign in with Google (if not already).
  2. Permanently delete notes you want removed (Trash → Delete forever, or Empty trash). This removes those note documents from your Google backup when signed in.
  3. Menu → Sign out.

Deleted: those notes from cloud backup; local data remains until you clear app data.

May remain in cloud: notebook metadata and any notes not permanently deleted; Firebase account record until you request full deletion (Option C).

Signing out alone does not delete cloud backup data.

C Request full account and cloud data deletion

Contact us via the Google Play developer page or MyNotes listing support with:

  • Subject: MyNotes account deletion
  • Your Google account email used to sign in
  • Confirmation that you want all MyNotes cloud backup data deleted

We will verify the request and, within 30 days, delete:

  • Firestore documents under your user backup (notebooks, notes, metadata)
  • The Firebase Authentication record associated with that sign-in

We cannot delete your Google account itself — manage that at Google Account.

— Kept / not controlled by us

  • Google Play purchase records for “Remove ads” — manage via Google Play
  • Ad/analytics data held by Google (AdMob, Firebase Analytics) per Google’s policies
  • Data on devices you have not cleared locally

— Retention

After a verified deletion request, we aim to complete cloud deletion within 30 days. Backups or logs we do not operate (Google/Firebase) follow Google’s retention rules.
