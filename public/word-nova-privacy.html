POLICY.

App: Word Nova: Search & Find Developer: Prasta Apps Studio Effective date: May 24, 2026 Last updated: May 24, 2026

Word Nova: Search & Find (“Word Nova,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a word search puzzle game for Android published by Prasta Apps Studio.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use the Word Nova mobile application (the “App”).

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

01 Information We Collect

1.1 Information stored on your device (not sent to our servers)

Word Nova does not require you to create an account. Most app data is stored locally on your device using Android’s on-device storage (Jetpack DataStore), including:

  • Game progress: category levels, puzzles completed, words found, scores, streaks, daily challenge status
  • Player profile: coins, XP, unlocked categories, purchased content flags
  • Statistics: play time, longest word, category progress, and similar gameplay stats
  • Settings: sound, music, vibration, font size, grid theme, colorblind mode, left-handed layout, notification preference, and other display/gameplay options

We do not operate our own backend servers to store this information. If you uninstall the App or clear app data, this information may be lost unless restored from a device backup.

Android backup: The App may allow Android’s backup/restore features (allowBackup is enabled). Backup content is handled by Google/your device manufacturer under their policies.

1.2 Information collected by third-party services

The App uses third-party services that may collect information automatically:

Google AdMob (advertising)

If you have not purchased “Remove Ads” or a premium option that disables ads, the App displays ads through Google AdMob, including:

  • Banner ads
  • Interstitial ads
  • Rewarded video ads
  • App open ads

AdMob may collect or receive information such as:

  • Advertising ID (AAID)
  • Device type, OS version, language, and general location (e.g., country/region inferred from IP)
  • App interactions with ads (impressions, clicks)
  • IP address and network-related data

The App declares the AD_ID permission and related ad services permissions required for advertising on modern Android versions.

For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Ads / AdMob.

You can limit ad personalization on your device via Settings → Google → Ads (wording may vary by device).

Google Play Billing (in-app purchases)

Purchases (coins, hint packs, category packs, Remove Ads, Premium Pass, etc.) are processed by Google Play. We do not collect or store your payment card or billing details.

Google may provide us with purchase confirmation and product identifiers so we can unlock content in the App. That entitlement is stored locally on your device.

See: Google Play Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

1.3 Information we do not collect

We do not knowingly collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or mailing address
  • User accounts or passwords
  • Precise GPS location
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data
  • Health or financial account information

The App does not include Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or similar analytics SDKs in the current version.

1.4 Share feature

The App may let you share daily puzzle results using Android’s standard share sheet (ACTION_SEND). You choose whether and with whom to share. We do not receive the content you share.

02 How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide and operate the game (progress, settings, statistics)
  • Process and restore in-app purchases and entitlements
  • Show ads and measure ad performance (via AdMob)
  • Improve stability and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

03 Legal Bases (EEA/UK users)

If you are in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, we process personal data based on:

  • Contract: to provide the App and purchased features
  • Legitimate interests: to operate, secure, and improve the App
  • Consent: where required for ads or notifications (you can withdraw consent in device or app settings)

04 Children’s Privacy

Word Nova is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the App, contact us via the Google Play developer page or the support option on the Word Nova listing, and we will take appropriate steps.

Parents and guardians can use device-level parental controls and Google Play purchase authentication.

05 Your Choices and Rights

All users

  • In-app settings: Adjust sound, vibration, notifications, and other preferences in Settings
  • Ads: Purchase Remove Ads / Premium (where available), or use device ad settings to limit personalization
  • Notifications: Enable or disable in Settings; Android 13+ also requires system notification permission
  • Delete data: Uninstall the App or clear storage via Android Settings → Apps → Word Nova → Storage

California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

You may have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected
  • Request deletion of personal information we maintain (if any)
  • Opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising

Because we do not operate a user account system, most data stays on your device or is handled by Google (ads/billing). For AdMob-related choices, use Google’s ad settings and, where offered, in-app ad consent tools required in your region.

To exercise rights, contact us via the Google Play developer page or Word Nova listing support.

EEA/UK residents (GDPR)

You may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object, and data portability where applicable. Contact us via the Play listing. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
