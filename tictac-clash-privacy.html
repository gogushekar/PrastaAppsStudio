PRIVACY
POLICY.

App: Tic Tac Clash Developer: Prasta Apps Studio Last updated: May 5, 2026

01 Overview

Tic Tac Clash is an offline tic-tac-toe game. We respect your privacy and designed the app to work without collecting personal information.

02 Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit personal information. The app does not require an account and does not connect to the internet for gameplay.

03 Information Stored on Your Device

The app saves local game statistics (wins and draws for X, O, and draws) using your device’s storage. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us or third parties.

Uninstalling the app removes locally stored statistics from your device.

04 Permissions

Tic Tac Clash may request the following permission:

  • Vibration — Used for haptic feedback when you tap cells or win a game.

We do not use this permission to collect personal data.

05 Third-Party Services

The app does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party data collection services.

06 Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

07 Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page will change when we do. Continued use of the app after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
